# Routing evidence guidance

Read only when reassessing routing. Provider task-fit descriptions and historical
measurements do not establish a general model, harness or price ranking. Compare
independently accepted real tasks with stable requirements and checks; retain
failures, retries, review, fixes and recovery. Keep API dollars, subscription
allowance, elapsed time, rework and cache-read volume distinct. Unknown cost stays
unknown; zero placeholders do not establish free usage.

Reuse valid evidence and suitable sessions. If comparison is necessary, require
authorization, matched inputs, repeat trials and stable acceptance gates; do not
launch a benchmark panel by default. Preserve writer isolation, independent review
and connector requirements regardless of any measurement.
